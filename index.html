The idea

Soup worth crossing the city for, made to take home.

Our story

Real soup from our neighborhood to yours.

It started in a family-run breakfast and lunch spot on a Chicago corner, where the soup pot is always on and the recipes come from the people behind the counter.

We're not a factory and we don't want to be. Boulevard Soup Co. grows only as fast as we can keep every batch tasting like home.

If it isn't good enough for our own counter, it doesn't go in a container.
