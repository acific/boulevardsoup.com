The idea

Soup worth crossing the city for, made to take home.

For years, the soup has been the reason people come back to a family-run breakfast and lunch spot on a Chicago corner. Boulevard Soup Co. takes the same recipes and the same care, makes them in small batches, and packs them so you can have a proper bowl on a Tuesday night.

How it's made

Made the slow way, on purpose.

No shortcuts and no factory line. The same three steps the soup has always taken at the counter, just with a lid at the end.

  1. 01

    From scratch

    Chopped, simmered and seasoned by hand, from the family's own recipes. Made the way it's made for the counter.

  2. 02

    Small batches

    A few pots at a time, so every batch gets tasted before it's ladled into a tub.

  3. 03

    Frozen at its best

    Chilled and frozen soon after cooking, so it's ready whenever you want a proper bowl.

Our story

Real soup from our boulevard to yours.

It started in a family-run breakfast and lunch spot on a Chicago corner, where the soup pot is always on and the recipes come from the people behind the counter.

We're not a factory and we don't want to be. Boulevard Soup Co. grows only as fast as we can keep every batch tasting like home.

If it isn't good enough for our own counter, it doesn't go in a container.
